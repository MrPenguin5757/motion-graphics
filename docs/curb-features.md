# Curb — Complete Feature List

Reference for the "All Features" website page and for ad copy/messaging.
Organized by area of the app.

## Home
- **Daily dashboard** — See stops today, expected revenue, and outstanding balance the moment you open the app.
- **Live weather bar** — Current forecast for your area shown right on the home screen, so you know what you're walking into before you leave the house.
- **Start today's route** — One tap opens your preferred navigation app (Apple Maps, Google Maps, or Waze) with every stop for the day loaded in order.
- **Today's run** — A live list of today's jobs with status, price, and payment state, updating as you work.
- **Getting Started checklist** — A short, dismissible checklist for new users covering the 5 things worth doing first — add a job, set up payment handles, customize your checklists, pick your services, style your invoices. Checks itself off as you go, and stays one tap away in Settings if you want it back.

## Jobs
- **List and calendar views** — Toggle between a day-grouped list or a full calendar view of every job.
- **Filter by All / Unpaid / Recurring** — Quickly narrow the list to what matters right now.
- **Weather-aware scheduling** — Each day's forecast shown right in the list, with rain advisory flags on jobs that fall within a trade's rain-sensitivity threshold.
- **Job creation in seconds** — Pick a client, pick a service, tap a date. Pricing fills in automatically based on service and vehicle/property size — edit if the job calls for something different.
- **Recurring jobs** — Flag a job as weekly, biweekly, or monthly so it's clearly marked as a repeat booking on your calendar.
- **Custom pricing** — Override the default price on any job with a one-tap reset back to standard if needed.
- **Service checklists** — Every service comes with a pre-built checklist (Full Detail has 9 steps, Gutter Clean-out has 5). Check items off as you go, or customize the list for how you actually work.
- **Photos** — Add before/after photos to any job from your camera or photo library.
- **Job notes** — Free-text notes on every job, with a visual indicator when notes exist.
- **Job timer** — Track time spent on each job for your own records and invoicing accuracy.
- **Signature capture** — Capture a client's signature right on your phone when a job is done.
- **Job completion summary** — A clear recap when you finish a job: time on site, checklist status, photo count, amount due — before you send the invoice.
- **Estimates sub-tab** — A dedicated view of every estimate you've sent, sorted by expiry or price, separate from your booked jobs.

## Estimates
- **Quick quotes** — Send a price estimate to someone before they're even a saved client — just their name, job address, and phone or email.
- **Attach to existing clients** — Or tie an estimate to someone already in your client list.
- **PDF estimates** — Generate a professional PDF quote in the same Clean, Bold, or Classic format as your invoices.
- **Status tracking** — Every estimate moves through Draft → Sent → Accepted → Converted (or Declined/Expired), so you always know where it stands.
- **Auto-expiry** — Estimates automatically mark themselves expired once their valid-until date passes — no manual cleanup.
- **Convert to job** — When a client says yes, convert the estimate straight into a scheduled job with pricing and date carried over.

## Route
- **Route optimization** — Curb orders your stops by proximity so you're not backtracking across town.
- **Drag to reorder** — Adjust the order yourself if you know a shortcut Curb doesn't.
- **One-tap navigation** — Start route opens your preferred navigation app with your whole day loaded, stop by stop.
- **Current-stop flow** — Step through your day one job at a time with a clear "Done, next stop" flow that advances job status as you go.
- **All-trades view** — See your route across every trade you run, not just one at a time.

## Clients
- **Full client records** — Name, phone, address, tags, gate code, access notes, preferred contact time, property details, email, and free-text notes — all in one place, and every section can be toggled on or off in Settings if you don't need it.
- **Custom fields** — Add your own fields to a client record beyond what's built in.
- **Tags** — Mark clients as Residential, Commercial, or HOA and filter by tag.
- **Search and sort** — Search by name, sort by name, balance, or most recent activity.
- **Payment handles** — Store each client's Venmo, Cash App, Zelle, or PayPal so you can request payment without asking every time.
- **Client balance and history** — See what a client owes and their full job history across every trade you do for them, right on their profile.
- **Estimates on file** — Any estimates sent to a client show right on their record alongside their job history.

## Money
- **Outstanding balance** — See exactly what you're owed, across every client, at a glance.
- **Collected and average ticket** — Track what you've brought in and your average job value.
- **Time filters** — View all-time totals or narrow to this month.
- **Sort who owes you** — Order your unpaid list by time outstanding (oldest debts first) or by amount (biggest first).
- **Who owes you** — A clear list of every unpaid job with one-tap actions: send an invoice, request payment, or mark it paid.
- **Payment requests** — Send a payment request via Venmo, Cash App, Zelle, or card with a pre-filled link — no manual typing.
- **Mark paid, your way** — Record how you got paid — cash, check (with check number), Venmo, Cash App, Zelle, PayPal, or card.

## Invoicing
- **Three invoice formats** — Clean (minimal white layout), Bold (dark header, high contrast), or Classic (traditional lined table) — pick the look that fits your brand.
- **PDF generation** — Every invoice generates as a real PDF you can share via text, email, AirDrop, or print.
- **Custom due dates** — Choose Receipt, Net 7, Net 14, or Net 30 per your business terms.
- **Tax rate** — Set your tax percentage once and it applies automatically.
- **Custom footer note** — Add your own "thank you" message or business note to every invoice.
- **Auto-numbering** — Every invoice gets a sequential number automatically.

## Weather
- **7-day forecast** — Powered by live weather data for your area, no separate app needed.
- **Calendar weather dots** — See at a glance which upcoming days have rain, storms, or clear skies.
- **Per-trade rain sensitivity** — Set how much rain matters for each trade you run — a pressure washer cares less about light rain than a detailer does.
- **Rain advisory flags** — Jobs scheduled during risky weather get flagged automatically so nothing catches you by surprise.

## Client Import
- **Import from Yardbook** — Export your customer list from Yardbook and bring it into Curb in one pass — no manual re-entry.
- **Import from any spreadsheet** — Upload a CSV from Google Sheets, Excel, or anywhere else, and match your columns to Curb's fields with a simple mapping screen.
- **Import from iPhone Contacts** — Use the native contacts picker to select exactly which contacts become clients — nothing pulled without your say-so.
- **Duplicate detection** — Already-existing clients are flagged during import so you never end up with the same person twice.
- **Preview before importing** — See every client that's about to be added, and deselect anything you don't want, before committing.

## Notifications
- **Job reminders** — Local reminders before a scheduled job, timed however you like.
- **En route text** — Auto-send a text to your client when you're on your way, with one tap.
- **Payment follow-ups** — Gentle reminders to follow up on unpaid invoices after a set number of days.

## Multiple Trades
- **Five built-in trades** — Auto Detailing, Lawn Care, Window Washing, Pressure Washing, and Gutter Cleaning, each with its own services, pricing, and checklists.
- **Custom trades** — Add your own trade type if you run something outside the built-in five.
- **All-trades mode** — See everything — jobs, route, revenue — across every trade you run, in one unified view.

## Shared Workspaces
- **Invite your team** — Create a shared workspace and invite others with a simple CRB-XXXX code.
- **Role-based access** — Assign Owner, Admin, or Crew roles. Crew members see only what's assigned to them — no client list, no financials, no settings.
- **Job assignment** — Assign any job to a specific team member and see who's doing what, today.
- **Manual and automatic sync** — Sync your team's data with a tap, or let it sync automatically as changes happen.
- **Fair data handling** — If a workspace subscription ever lapses, everyone keeps their own data on their own phone. Nobody loses anything.

## Crew Timeclock
- **Clock in and out** — Crew members clock in and out right from their assigned job (or on their own for general hours), with the address recorded at each end — not continuous tracking, just a record of when and where.
- **Today's activity** — Workspace owners see who's clocked in, where, and for how long, all in one view — plus in/out times and location for everyone's most recent shift.
- **Weekly hours and pay** — Set an hourly rate per team member and see their weekly hours and earnings calculated automatically — simple enough to hand-pay via Venmo, no full payroll system needed.

## Settings & Customization
- **Business profile** — Your business name and logo, shown on every invoice and estimate.
- **Checklist defaults** — Customize the default checklist for every service you offer.
- **Job defaults** — Set your default payment method, standard time slots, and recurring job intervals.
- **Appearance** — Full light and dark mode, plus currency and distance unit preferences.
- **Navigation app preference** — Choose Apple Maps, Google Maps, or Waze for all in-app directions.
- **Location & weather settings** — Set your ZIP code and adjust rain sensitivity per trade.
- **PIN lock** — Add a PIN to keep the app locked when you're not using it.
- **Backup & restore** — Back up your data on demand and save it to iCloud Drive, AirDrop, or email — restore it on any device whenever you need to.
- **Data export** — Export everything — jobs, clients, settings — as a file, and merge an exported file back in later.
- **In-app hints** — Quiet, one-time tips that show up at the right moment — like a nudge to add payment handles or invite your crew — never repeated once you've seen them.
- **Clear all data** — A full reset if you ever want to start fresh.
